randoo privacy policies

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

The short version: randoo apps have no accounts, no analytics, and no server to talk to. Nothing you do in an app is sent to us. Where an app shows ads, they are served by Google AdMob, and where that involves personal data it is Google – acting on its own – that handles it, under its own policy and with your consent where the law requires it.

randoo is a small project operated by Merandoo.

Choose your app
