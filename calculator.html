Privacy Policy

Applies to this app. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

The short version: This app has no accounts, no analytics, and no server to talk to. Nothing you do in this app is sent to us. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and where that involves personal data, it is Google – acting on its own – that handles it, under its own policy and with your consent where the law requires it.

1. Who we are

This policy covers Calculator only. It is published by an independent developer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google.

Each app in this collection has its own policy, because each is a separate app with its own listing and its own data practices.

2. What the app stores

This app stores a small amount of information on your device only, using Android's private app storage:

  • Settings – theme, language, haptics and sound preferences.
  • History – in Coin Flip, the last 100 flips, so they are still there when you reopen the app.

This data never leaves your device. We operate no servers, have no access to it, and cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Analytics

There is none. This app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry SDK, and collect no usage statistics. We do not know how often any app is used, by whom, or how it is used.

4. Advertising

This app shows no ads.

When an ad is requested, Google – not us – may process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • device model, operating system and app version;
  • your approximate location, usually derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was viewed or clicked.

We do not receive this information, we cannot see it, and we are not a party to that processing. Google's handling of it is described in Google's partner site policy, and you can review or reset your advertising ID at any time in Google My Ad Center or in your device's privacy settings.

5. Who we share data with

We share nothing, because we collect nothing. Our only external relationship is that Google AdMob serves ads; Google's role is as an independent party, governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Legal bases (GDPR/UK GDPR)

  • App operation on your device: legitimate interest in operating a free app, balanced against your rights and freedoms.
  • On-device settings and history: never transmitted, so no processing takes place.

7. Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we are aware of none of their users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: device settings, or Google My Ad Center.
  • Change your consent choice: clear the app's storage in Android settings, which resets the consent record so the form is shown again next launch.
  • Delete all local data: uninstall the app.

Because we hold no data about you, we cannot export or delete it – there is none.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date before it takes effect. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
