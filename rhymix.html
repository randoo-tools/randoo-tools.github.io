Privacy Policy

Applies to Rhymix, package com.rhymix.app. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

The short version: This app has no accounts, no analytics, and no server to talk to. Your saved words, round history, and settings stay on your device. If you use optional voice input, your device's speech recognition service processes that audio. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and where that involves personal data, it is Google – acting on its own – that handles it, under its own policy and with your consent where the law requires it.

1. Who we are

This policy covers Rhymix only. It is published by an independent developer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google.

Each app in this collection has its own policy, because each is a separate app with its own listing and its own data practices.

2. What the app stores

This app stores information on your device only, using its private app storage:

  • Settings – theme, interface language, learning language, and sound preferences.
  • Saved vocabulary – words you bookmark during play so you can review them later.
  • Round history and game counter – past round summaries and a local counter of games started so early rounds remain ad-free.

The app does not send your settings, saved words, or round history to us. We operate no servers, have no access to this data, and cannot see it. Depending on your device and backup settings, its backup service may include app data in a device backup. Deleting the app removes its local data.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Rhymix offers optional voice input (speech-to-text) during a round and requests the device microphone permission only when you choose to use it. When voice input is active, your spoken audio is handled by your device's built-in speech recognition service (such as Google Speech Services on Android or Apple Speech Recognition on iOS) to transcribe your word into text. We do not record, store, or receive any audio.

4. Analytics

There is none. This app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry SDK, and collects no usage statistics. We do not know how often this app is used, by whom, or how it is used.

5. Advertising

Rhymix shows ads served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google – not us – may process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • device model, operating system and app version;
  • your approximate location, usually derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was viewed or clicked;
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the advertising SDK perform, such as launch time and energy usage.

We do not receive this information, we cannot see it, and we are not a party to that processing. Google's handling of it is described in Google's partner site policy, and you can review or reset your advertising ID at any time in Google My Ad Center or in your device's privacy settings.

6. Advertising consent

Where the law requires it – including in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland – the app presents Google's User Messaging Platform consent form before personalised ads can be shown. If you decline, or dismiss the form without agreeing, you continue to receive ads, but they are non-personalised and earn less. Declining has no effect on the app's features, which are unaffected either way.

7. Who we share data with

We do not collect or receive your saved words, round history, settings, or voice audio. Google AdMob serves ads and, if you enable voice input, your device's speech recognition provider processes spoken audio; each acts as an independent party governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

8. Legal bases (GDPR/UK GDPR)

  • Personalised advertising: your consent, obtained through the consent form described above.
  • Non-personalised advertising and basic app operation: legitimate interest in operating a free app, balanced against your rights and freedoms.
  • On-device settings, saved vocabulary, and round history: the app does not transmit them to us. Your device's backup service may handle them if backups are enabled.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: device settings, or Google My Ad Center.
  • Change your consent choice: clear the app's storage in Android settings, which resets the consent record so the form is shown again next launch.
  • Revoke microphone access: disable the microphone permission for Rhymix in your device settings at any time.
  • Delete all local data: uninstall the app.

Because we hold no data about you, we cannot export or delete it – there is none.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date before it takes effect. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
